Richer Ablations: Rollout / Canary & Risk Scorer

This ablation compares immediate deployment (no canary), full canary pipeline, and risk scorer simplification. Metrics include ASR, benign success, regressions, and bad patches shipped. Results show that canary and multi-objective risk scoring reduce regressions and bad patches, while immediate deployment increases risk. See rollout_ablation.csv and rollout_ablation_summary.txt for full results and explanation.
