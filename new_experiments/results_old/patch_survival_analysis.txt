Patch Survival Analysis (RQ3 – Adaptivity)

Survival variable: For each patch, time = number of adaptive attack rounds or adversarial queries until first successful bypass (right-censored if not bypassed).

Survival curves: Fraction of patches not yet bypassed vs. rounds. Compared: Full AutoPatch-Lite vs. weakened variant (no RL refinement).

Statistical comparison: Log-rank test used to compare survival distributions.

Interpretation: AutoPatch-Lite shows longer patch survival under adaptive attacks, indicating greater robustness. See patch_survival_curve.csv and patch_survival_curve.png for details.
