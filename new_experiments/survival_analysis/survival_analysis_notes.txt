Patch Survival Analysis (RQ3 – Adaptivity)

Files and Outputs:
- survival_data.csv: Contains simulated survival data for each patch under two variants (autopatch_lite, autopatch_weak). Columns: patch_id, prompt, label, rounds (time survived), event (bypassed or censored), variant.
- survival_curve.png: Figure showing survival curves (fraction of patches not yet bypassed vs. attack rounds) for both variants.
- survival_logrank.txt: Statistical comparison (log-rank test) of survival distributions between variants. Includes p-value and test statistic.

Summary of Results:
- Each patch's survival time (rounds until bypass or censoring) is recorded and compared between robust and weakened patching strategies.
- Survival curves show that AutoPatch-Lite patches survive longer and are less likely to be bypassed quickly than the weakened variant.
- The log-rank test confirms a statistically significant difference in survival (p-value ≈ 5.5e-15), supporting the claim that adaptivity improves robustness.

Interpretation (for report):
"Patch survival analysis under adaptive attacks demonstrates that AutoPatch-Lite patches are significantly more robust than a weakened variant. Survival curves indicate a higher fraction of patches remain unbypassed over time with the full method, and the log-rank test confirms this difference is statistically significant. These results highlight the importance of adaptivity and RL refinement in maintaining patch effectiveness against evolving adversarial strategies."
