J3: Targeted Mitigation Evaluation Methods Note

Targeted mitigation results (Table J3) evaluate interface-scoped slices of the harmful dataset, not the global harmful universe. The Tool-argument constraint/repair mitigation operates on a tool-misuse slice (n=49 harmful, n=17 benign); the RAG context sanitization mitigation operates on a RAG-injection slice (n=68 harmful, n=36 benign). Evaluations are conducted by replaying these cached harmful and benign examples against the patch-defended systems, rather than sampling new production paths or all possible request patterns. The patch apply-rate is defined as the fraction of attempts where the patch mechanism successfully activated (tool_invocation_attempted for tool constraints; retrieval_view_extraction for RAG sanitization). When patches do not apply, the mismatch_histogram attributes indicate the distribution of failure reasons: MATCH_FALSE indicates the detection predicate did not trigger, SCOPE_MISMATCH indicates the request fell outside the intended surface, and other categories (TOOL_NAME_MISMATCH, ARG_SCHEMA_NOT_PRESENT, RETRIEVAL_TEXT_EMPTY, RETRIEVAL_TEXT_NORMALIZATION_MISMATCH) indicate structural misalignments. Benign pass-rate and harmful ASR are computed only over the respective slice; targeted results do not directly generalize to the full harmful set but demonstrate controlled effectiveness within their defined scope.
